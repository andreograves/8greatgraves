The first hundred years

From Freeman's birth to the passing of Daniel, the last of the eight.

  1. 1849
    Freeman Graves is born in Mississippi.
  2. 1859
    Eliza Price is born in Mississippi.
  3. 1870
    Laura, the first of the eight, is born in Collins.
  4. 1897
    Daniel, the youngest, is born. The eight are complete.
  5. 1967
    John Freeman and Jasper pass in Collins.
  6. 1982
    Daniel, the last of the eight on record, passes in Collins.
